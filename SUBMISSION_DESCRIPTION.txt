BountyMatchRouter: a skill-matching escrow, not a duplicate-detection gate. A poster deposits a reward against a plain-text requirement; workers self-register a skill summary into a pool. find_and_judge deterministically ranks the pool by embedding similarity, then asks validators one prompt_comparative round whether the closest untried candidate's stated skills genuinely fit the requirement. Only FIT proposes a match; escrow pays on confirmation, refunds on cancellation or timeout. Review caught two lifecycle bugs: re-registering appended to the stored embedding instead of replacing it, zeroing future ranking; confirm_match skipped its own deadline check, relying on a separate reclaim call first.
