BountyMatchRouter: a skill-matching escrow, not a duplicate-detection gate. A poster deposits a reward against a plain-text requirement; workers self-register a free-text skill summary into a pool. find_and_judge deterministically ranks the pool by embedding similarity (zero nondet cost), then asks validators one prompt_comparative round whether the closest untried candidate's stated skills genuinely fit the requirement, never a bare similarity threshold. Only FIT proposes a match; escrow pays on confirmation, refunds on cancellation or timeout, never stranded. Deployed and fully exercised on StudioNet at 0x6Dd70aF244C9766D29Cb57545fc445C7a3D60d5e: a live judgement correctly returned FIT for a matching skill summary, and two independent instances converged on the identical fit band. 49/49 direct-mode adversarial tests pass; lint clean. Two real bugs surfaced only on StudioNet (a missing .view() call, a gltest path issue) and were fixed before submission.
